How much to trust the backtest

  • Point in time: each list uses only quotes, filings and events public on its date, through the same code as the live screen; share counts are the ones filed at the time.
  • Not in the numbers: brokerage, taxes, slippage. A yearly rebalance trades little, but taxes on gains are real.
  • Survivors: companies that went bankrupt or into recovery stay in, and their quotes follow them into the special segments. Companies that delisted keep their quotes but lose their dividends (B3 only serves listed companies' history), which slightly understates returns.
  • Approximations before 2020: share counts come from the yearly Reference Form, dated by when CVM received it; registration status is today's, so it is not used as a filter in the past.
  • One path: fifteen yearly decisions are a small sample. A strategy that beat the market here can lag for years; compare it with "all eligible stocks" to see what the picking itself added.

Track record

How it is kept

  • Every Monday the top 10 by score (and by the Magic Formula) is appended to data/picks/picks.csv with its prices. Nothing is ever removed.
  • Each list is held as an equal-weight portfolio until the next one, on dividend- and split-adjusted prices, against BOVA11 over the same days.
  • Lists are stamped with the scoring rules' fingerprint: a rule change ends one record and starts another, it never rewrites the past.

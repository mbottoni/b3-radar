How much to trust the backtest

  • Point in time: each list uses only quotes, filings and events public on its date, through the same code as the live screen.
  • Not in the numbers: brokerage, taxes, slippage. A yearly rebalance trades little, but taxes on gains are real.
  • Survivors: companies that went bankrupt or in recovery stay in, and their quotes follow them into the special segments. Companies that delisted keep their quotes but lose their dividends (B3 only serves listed companies' history), which slightly understates returns.
  • Approximations before 2020: share counts come from the yearly Reference Form, checked against B3's count carried back through splits; registration status is today's, so it is not used as a filter in the past.
  • One path: fifteen yearly decisions are a small sample. A strategy that beat the market here can lag for years; compare it with "all eligible stocks" to see what the picking itself added.
